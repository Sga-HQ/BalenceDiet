BalenceDiet — Vision
BalenceDiet turns a shared weekly food menu (like a hostel mess) into personalised meal and portion guidance for each user.

Who it is for
Anyone eating from a fixed, shared menu. Built and tested with one user first, but nothing is hardcoded to one person, institution, or cuisine.

What it does
Reads the weekly menu and understands each dish
Knows each user's goal, diet type, activity level, allergies and deficiencies
Tells the user what to eat and roughly how much, in practical units (e.g. "3 eggs + 3–4 spoons rice")
What it does NOT do
It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions
It does not generate recipes or do grocery shopping
It does not claim exact nutrition: all values are estimates
